# Talk About Food: new phrases from Tatoeba (draft for review)

Made on 2026-10-31 using the Samanantar-style method: open sentences from Tatoeba, matched Marathi ↔ Hindi through English, then filtered for short, everyday food sentences.

- **Source:** [Tatoeba.org](https://tatoeba.org) export of 2026-10-31. Licence **CC BY 2.0 FR**. We must credit Tatoeba (a Credits page) and keep the sentence IDs.
- **What came out:** 88,179 Marathi sentences; 7,700 of them have a Hindi match (3,387 direct, the rest through English). After the food + "7 words or fewer" filter: 5,143 candidates. I picked the ones below by hand.
- **Hindi column:** "Tatoeba" = taken from Tatoeba; "Draft" = written by Claude because Tatoeba had no Hindi. Drafts need checking.
- **Transliteration:** written in the app's style (Kiti vajta?, Mala chaha aavadto).

**How to review:** for each row, mark ✅ keep, ✏️ change (write the fix) or ❌ drop. A native Marathi speaker should check the Marathi; you can check the Hindi.

Each lesson has 3 phrases today. With these, a lesson would have about 8.

## Things I noticed

- **One person wrote almost all of Tatoeba's Marathi:** 88,092 of 88,179 sentences are by the same contributor. Most read well, but I skipped some with mistakes, e.g. "मी तहोनलेलो आहे" (should be तहानलेलो), "गुळ" (should be गूळ), "बिलचं" (should be बिलाचं), and a sentence where Marathi "तिने" (she) was paired with English "He".
- **Lots of noise:** Western names (Tom, Mary: 642 of the candidates), foreign food (daikon, jelly, cheesecake), and keyword mix-ups ("Pay attention!" matched "pay"). Those were filtered out.
- **Existing transliterations need fixing:** several current phrases write the final ं as "m", e.g. *havam* (हवं), *dilam* (दिलं), *taazam* (ताजं), *veglam*, *gharcham*, *khanam*. In speech these end in a plain vowel: *hava*, *dila*, *taaza*. Worth fixing across the module.
