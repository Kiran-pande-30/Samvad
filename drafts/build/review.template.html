Out And About, version 2 बाहेर फिरूया

Asking the way, giving directions in a rickshaw, the bus, and finding a place. 5 lessons of 5 sentences, each practised 3 ways, with Hindi prompts and options, the same format as Essentials and Talk About Food. It replaces today's Out And About (20 short phrases, mostly English prompts).

  1. Check the main sentences of a lesson and flag anything that reads wrong.
  2. Press Send to Claude when you've finished a round of flags. Claude fixes them, marks them Fixed and replies in the comment thread.
  3. Approve the lesson once it is right.
  4. Move to the next lesson. The steps sit under each lesson if you want to look.

What to check

  • Marathi phrases: natural, everyday Pune Marathi. Phrases marked "New" come from Tatoeba, where one person wrote almost all the Marathi.
  • Wrong options: clearly wrong for the situation, but not silly. A wrong option that is also a correct answer is a bug.
  • Hindi: the meanings and options use everyday Hindi in the आप form where the Marathi is polite.
  • He/she forms: Marathi verbs change with the speaker (मी ठीक आहे is neutral; मी आलो / मी येतो are not). Flag any that assume the learner is a man.
  • Scene text is stored like today's lessons but the app does not show it yet.
  • Directions: in the rickshaw lessons the learner gives the directions, so सरळ चला and डावीकडे वळा are the learner's own lines.

How each lesson is built

  • Phrase 1: match, fill the blank, conversation
  • Phrases 2 and 4: match, arrange, recall
  • Phrase 3: fill the blank, conversation, recall
  • Phrase 5: fill the blank, arrange, conversation
  • Step order: match, fill and arrange are mixed sentence by sentence (each sentence is matched before you fill or arrange it), then conversation, then recall.

After the review

  1. Claude applies the fixes and re-checks every step.
  2. The old Out And About is backed up, then replaced with these 5 lessons. Nobody has progress in it yet.
  3. Audio is generated for the new sentences.
